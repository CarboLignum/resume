AI Job Agent

Task

Automate the search and processing of large volumes of job vacancies and identify those that correspond to my actual professional capabilities.

What I Built

The system retrieves vacancies and their full content from external sources, collects and structures the data, performs local filtering and semantic analysis of the content.

Suitable vacancies are then passed to a separate employer outreach process.

What the System Does

The system can process thousands of vacancies every day, automatically filter out irrelevant ones, analyse the remaining vacancies, select suitable opportunities and pass them to the next outreach process.

The outreach process takes available channels into account — forms and email — together with the history of previous actions and protection against repeated outreach for the same vacancy.

AI is one component of the working process alongside data collection, processing rules, automation and state management.
